# Adverbs & Prepositions

**Part of Speech:** Adverbs & Prepositions

ক্রিয়া বিশেষণ ও Preposition — প্রতিটি শব্দের অর্থ ও example sentence দেখে বাস্তব কথোপকথনে ব্যবহার করো।

## Learning Objectives
- Understand each word and its Bengali meaning.
- Recognize how the word works in a sentence.
- Use the words in everyday English.

## Vocabulary List

| English | বাংলা অর্থ | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **nearby** | কাছাকাছি | Is there a pharmacy nearby? |
| **straight** | সোজা | Go straight for two minutes. |
| **behind** | পেছনে | The shop is behind the bank. |
| **opposite** | বিপরীতে | The hotel is opposite the mall. |
| **between** | মাঝখানে | The cafe is between two shops. |

## Practice
1. Choose 10 words and recall their meanings without looking.
2. Write 5 original English sentences using these words.
3. Read the example sentences aloud.

## Self-check
- [ ] I can remember at least 80% of the meanings.
- [ ] I can use at least 5 words in my own sentences.
- [ ] I can recognize these words in everyday conversation.
