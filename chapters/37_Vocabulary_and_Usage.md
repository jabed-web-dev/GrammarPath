# Vocabulary and Usage Reference

## Grammar-aware vocabulary learning
শব্দ আলাদা করে নয়, তার part of speech, countability, verb pattern এবং collocation-সহ শেখো।

| Word | Part of speech | Meaning | Typical pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| advice | uncountable noun | পরামর্শ | a piece of advice | She gave me a piece of advice. |
| information | uncountable noun | তথ্য | information about/on | I need information about the course. |
| depend | verb | নির্ভর করা | depend on | It depends on the weather. |
| explain | verb | ব্যাখ্যা করা | explain something to someone | Explain the rule to me. |
| suggest | verb | প্রস্তাব করা | suggest doing / suggest that... | She suggested leaving early. |
| interested | adjective | আগ্রহী | interested in | I am interested in science. |
| good | adjective | ভালো | good at | He is good at maths. |
| arrive | verb | পৌঁছানো | arrive at/in | We arrived at the airport. |
| discuss | verb | আলোচনা করা | discuss something | Let's discuss the plan. |
| prefer | verb | বেশি পছন্দ করা | prefer A to B | I prefer tea to coffee. |

## Countability reminders
- Uncountable: advice, information, furniture, equipment, luggage, homework, news, research (সাধারণ ব্যবহার)।
- Say: some advice, a piece of information, two items of furniture.
- Countable: suggestion, fact, chair, device, bag, assignment.

## Collocation notebook template
প্রতিটি নতুন শব্দের জন্য লিখবে: (1) বাংলা অর্থ, (2) part of speech, (3) countable/uncountable, (4) verb/preposition pattern, (5) একটি নিজের উদাহরণ, (6) উচ্চারণ।
