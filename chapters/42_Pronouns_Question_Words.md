# Pronouns & Question Words

**Part of Speech:** Pronouns & Question Words

সর্বনাম ও প্রশ্নবোধক শব্দ — প্রতিটি শব্দের অর্থ ও example sentence দেখে বাস্তব কথোপকথনে ব্যবহার করো।

## Learning Objectives
- Understand each word and its Bengali meaning.
- Recognize how the word works in a sentence.
- Use the words in everyday English.

## Vocabulary List

| English | বাংলা অর্থ | Example Sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | কী | What is your name? |
| **Where** | কোথায় | Where do you live? |
| **When** | কখন | When will you arrive? |
| **Why** | কেন | Why are you late? |
| **Who** | কে | Who is calling? |
| **Whose** | কার | Whose phone is this? |
| **Which** | কোনটি | Which one do you prefer? |
| **How** | কীভাবে / কেমন | How does this work? |
| **How much** | কত দাম / কত পরিমাণ | How much is this? |
| **How many** | কতগুলো | How many people are coming? |
| **How long** | কতক্ষণ / কত লম্বা | How long will it take? |
| **How far** | কত দূর | How far is the station? |
| **What time** | কয়টা / কখন | What time does it open? |

## Practice
1. Choose 10 words and recall their meanings without looking.
2. Write 5 original English sentences using these words.
3. Read the example sentences aloud.

## Self-check
- [ ] I can remember at least 80% of the meanings.
- [ ] I can use at least 5 words in my own sentences.
- [ ] I can recognize these words in everyday conversation.
