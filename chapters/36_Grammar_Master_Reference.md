# Grammar Master Reference

## Verb phrase quick patterns
| Pattern | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Simple present | V1/V-s | She works. |
| Present continuous | am/is/are + V-ing | She is working. |
| Present perfect | have/has + V3 | She has worked. |
| Past simple | V2 | She worked. |
| Past continuous | was/were + V-ing | She was working. |
| Past perfect | had + V3 | She had worked. |
| Future | will + V1 | She will work. |
| Passive | be + V3 | It was made. |
| Modal | modal + V1 | She can work. |
| Modal perfect | modal + have + V3 | She may have left. |

## Core sentence patterns
- S + V: Birds fly.
- S + V + C: She is happy.
- S + V + O: I read books.
- S + V + IO + DO: He gave me a key.
- S + V + O + C: They named her captain.

## Question / negative patterns
- be/modal/perfect auxiliary: invert auxiliary and subject.
- no auxiliary in present/past simple: use do/does/did + V1.
- negative: auxiliary + not; otherwise do-support.

## Agreement reminders
- The head of the subject phrase controls the verb in most cases.
- each/every + singular noun → singular verb.
- a number of + plural noun → plural; the number of → singular.
- fractions/percentages usually agree with the noun after of.
- either...or / neither...nor often follow the nearer subject.

## Preposition quick reference
- at 7; on Monday; in July / 2026.
- at the station; in a room/city; on a surface.
- since + starting point; for + duration; by + deadline; until + endpoint.

## Editing checklist
1. Is there a clear subject and finite verb?
2. Does the verb agree with its subject?
3. Is tense/aspect consistent with the time meaning?
4. Is the verb pattern correct (V1, V2, V3, V-ing, to-infinitive)?
5. Are articles and countability correct?
6. Are modifiers placed beside what they modify?
7. Are clauses joined and punctuated correctly?
